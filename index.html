Good questions, honest answers.

A practical guide to making greener choices.

Do I need to buy eco-friendly products?

No. Using what you already own, reducing waste, repairing items and changing daily habits can be excellent places to start. Buy a replacement only when you need one.

How do you choose recommendations?

The site uses your selected habits, budget and priorities to rank practical ideas. Recommendations are general guidance, not a personalized lifecycle assessment or a guarantee of a specific carbon saving.

Are all sustainable products better?

Not automatically. Materials, durability, manufacturing, transport and how often a product is reused all matter. Avoid replacing a usable item solely to buy a greener-looking version.

What if an idea does not suit my life?

Skip it. Consider your safety, accessibility, dietary needs and local options, and choose an alternative that is realistic for you.

What happens to my preferences?

This lightweight version does not send your profile to a server. Preferences and checklist progress are saved in your browser when storage is available. Use the reset option to clear this site's saved preferences.

How reliable are environmental benefits?

Benefits vary by location and circumstances. The site explains likely directions of impact and avoids promising exact emissions savings without enough activity data and appropriate emission factors.
